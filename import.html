One step left — deal with the original file

Those leads are now inside Datafort, masked and assigned. But the spreadsheet you just imported is still sitting on your computer, unprotected, and it can still be emailed, copied to a pendrive, or opened by anyone who borrows the machine.

Until that file is gone, Datafort is protecting a copy while the original walks around freely. This is the single most common way a secured lead list still leaks.
