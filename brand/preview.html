Alert red and brand red are the same hue and cannot be told apart by colour alone. They are separated by treatment: brand red takes solid fills (primary button, active nav), alert red takes tints, outlines and transient toasts only. A destructive button rebuilt as a solid red fill becomes indistinguishable from Save.

The knockout variant is not optional. The inner figure and wordmark in logo.png are near-black — on dark chrome they vanish, as the marked panel above shows. Use logo-light.png / logo-mark-light.png on anything dark.
