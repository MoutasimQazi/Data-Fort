Revocation is permanent. A revoked device cannot be reactivated — you will have to issue a new certificate. Live sessions on this laptop end immediately.

This blocks the device inside Datafort. The certificate itself stays cryptographically valid until you also revoke it at the CA and publish the CRL. Until you do, a stolen laptop can still reach the login page — it just cannot get past it.
